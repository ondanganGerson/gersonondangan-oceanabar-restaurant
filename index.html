About OCEANA

A welcoming coastal table in Bacong.

OCEANA Bar and Restaurant is a relaxed, friendly dining spot tucked along the shores of Bacong, Negros Oriental. We bring together the freshest local catch, flame-grilled favorites, and classic Filipino comfort dishes — all served with warm hospitality and an easygoing island vibe.

Whether you're stopping by for a long family meal, a casual gathering with friends, or just a quiet drink by the sea, OCEANA is open for dine-in and takeout — and proud to be an LGBTQ+ friendly space where everyone is welcome.

  • Fresh local seafood
  • Grilled to order
  • Family-friendly atmosphere
  • LGBTQ+ welcoming
